Ragnar M keeps your machines running.

It parks at a machine, unloads the finished part and loads the next one from its cassettes, cycle after cycle. When that machine is covered, the same robot can drive to the next one, swap print plates or move parts between stations. You do not need an arm bolted to each machine.

  1. Takes a block of stock from its cassette
  2. Loads it into the vise
  3. Backs out while the door closes and the machine cuts
  4. Puts the finished part in its own cassette

Ragnar C does the same for walls.

It runs on the same base as Ragnar M, with a mast in place of the arm riser. Its arm picks its own tools from the deck, and the mast lifts it to the top of the wall. Ragnar C rolls paint in lanes, cuts the ceiling line, drills, sands and tapes, then sends you photos of every wall.

  1. Paint roller, on the arm
  2. Drill
  3. Sander
  4. Tape head
  5. Gripper
