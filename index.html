Robot technicians for the jobs nobody wants to repeat

Mobile robots that change their own tools. They load machines, swap print plates, move parts and paint walls, in shops, data centers and buildings. We set up the whole job.

R1 takes the repetitive work off your crew.

Its arm picks its own tools from the deck, and a mast lifts it to the top of the wall. R1 rolls paint in lanes, cuts the ceiling line, drills, sands and tapes, then sends you photos of every wall.

How it works

Every job runs in four steps.

Scan
The robot maps the space and finds every machine, rack, wall and door it will work around.
Plan
It plans the job from the scan: parts to load, plates to swap, lanes to paint, holes to drill.
Work
It picks the right tool, does the work and checks the result with a camera as it goes.
Report
You get photos and a log of everything it did.

One family, three jobs

R1 finishes walls in buildings. R2 tends machines in shops and print farms. Mini works the racks in data centers. R1 and R2 share one chassis with swappable tops. Mini is the compact one for tight aisles.
