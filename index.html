Robot technicians for buildings

Ragnar Robotics builds robots that paint, drill and sand inside buildings, so your crew finishes more rooms with less labor.

Get in touch

Meet R1

One robot for the light, repetitive work inside a building. Its arm picks its own tools from the deck, and a mast lifts it to the top of the wall.

Paints

Rolls the wall in lanes and cuts the ceiling line, with paint fed from a sealed tank.

Drills and sands

Swaps to a drill, a sander or a taping head on its own and catches the dust.

Reaches high

A three stage mast lifts the arm to 16 ft walls, so nobody is on a ladder.

Reports back

Photos of every wall and a list of what was done land on your phone.

R1 is in development and has not been built yet. The images are CAD renderings, and the numbers come from our simulations. See R1 up close

How it works

  1. Scan

    R1 maps the room and finds every wall, corner, outlet, door and window.

  2. Plan

    It plans the job from the scan and the drawings: lanes to paint, holes to drill, patches to sand.

  3. Work

    It picks the right tool, does the work and checks the result with a camera as it goes.

  4. Report

    You get photos of every wall and a list of what was done.

Short on painters?

Labor is 75 to 95 percent of the cost of a professional interior paint job, and painters are hard to hire. R1 is built to take the slow, repetitive part off your crew.
