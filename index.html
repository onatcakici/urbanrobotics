Robot technicians for buildings

Ragnar Robotics builds robots that paint, drill and sand inside buildings, so your crew finishes more rooms with less labor.

Get in touchSee R1

R1 does the light, repetitive work inside a building.

Its arm picks its own tools from the deck, and a mast lifts it to the top of the wall. R1 rolls paint in lanes, cuts the ceiling line, drills, sands and tapes, then sends you photos of every wall.

R1 is in development and has not been built yet. The images are CAD renderings, and the numbers come from our simulations.

How it works

Scan
R1 maps the room and finds every wall, corner, outlet, door and window.
Plan
It plans the job from the scan and the drawings: lanes to paint, holes to drill, patches to sand.
Work
It picks the right tool, does the work and checks the result with a camera as it goes.
Report
You get photos of every wall and a list of what was done.

Short on painters?

Labor is 75 to 95 percent of the cost of a professional interior paint job, and painters are hard to hire. R1 is built to take the slow, repetitive part off your crew.
