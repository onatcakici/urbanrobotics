How it works

Every job runs in four steps.

Scan
The robot maps the space and finds every machine, rack, wall and door it will work around.
Plan
It plans the job from the scan: parts to load, plates to swap, lanes to paint, holes to drill.
Work
It picks the right tool, does the work and checks the result with a camera as it goes.
Report
You get photos and a log of everything it did.
