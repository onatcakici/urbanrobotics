R1 takes the repetitive work off your crew.

Its arm picks its own tools from the deck, and a mast lifts it to the top of the wall. R1 rolls paint in lanes, cuts the ceiling line, drills, sands and tapes, then sends you photos of every wall.

How it works

Every job runs in four steps.

Scan
The robot maps the space and finds every machine, rack, wall and door it will work around.
Plan
It plans the job from the scan: parts to load, plates to swap, lanes to paint, holes to drill.
Work
It picks the right tool, does the work and checks the result with a camera as it goes.
Report
You get photos and a log of everything it did.

Where they work

One robot base, with different tools for each job. R1 is in development, the technician for shops and print farms is in design, and we are taking pilot requests for both now.

Machine shops
Loads raw parts, unloads finished ones and keeps the machines cutting between shifts.
Print farms
Swaps build plates and clears finished parts, so each printer starts its next job without waiting for someone.
Data centers
Swaps drives and checks racks in the server hall, around the clock. Planned.
Buildings
R1 paints, sands, drills and tapes walls and ceilings. In development now.
