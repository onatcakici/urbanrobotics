Robots for the finishing trades

We start with paint. Urban Robotics is a robotics company in Miami, and its first product is P2, an interior painting robot for homes. P2 is in development: the CAD design is done, and bench tests on real walls come next.

Why paint

Painting is slow, physical work that every building needs: ladders, tape, plastic sheets and long days, and most of the bill is labor. Our first design, P1, was a spray robot for commercial interiors. Working on it showed us that a home is different: the furniture stays, and spray means masking everything. So P2 is designed to roll, the way a painter does, and to carry its tools on one arm: paint first, with spray, sand and drill planned for the same Tool Port.

We design it from standard parts, and we write down how it can fail before we build it. See how P2 is designed to paint a room.

Founder

Onat Cakici

Founder and engineer

Onat designs P2 from end to end: the CAD model, the painting simulation and the failure analysis. He studied aerospace engineering at the Florida Institute of Technology (B.S., 2025) and has built machines since he was 11.

Before Urban Robotics he led a nine engineer team that built RangerEye, an autonomous drone for spotting wildfires early, and in high school he helped build a rescue arm that was used alongside firefighters in Istanbul. He works across mechanical design, electronics, autonomy and the shop floor. He is also a three time Turkish national champion in rowing.

Before P2

Machines Onat built before Urban Robotics, on his own and with teams. These are real builds, so the pictures are photos, not renders.

  1. RangerEye

    2024 to 2025

    An autonomous VTOL drone for spotting wildfires early in remote areas. Onat led the nine engineer team at Florida Tech and worked on its autonomy, flight tests, charging and ground station. The airframe is 3D printed in under 24 hours, and the ground station launches, recovers and recharges it. More at rangereye.org.

  2. Rescue arm

    2017

    A telescoping arm with a soft gripper, built in three hours with his FIRST team when a puppy was trapped in a narrow well in Istanbul. The arm was used alongside firefighters to bring the puppy out, a rescue covered by Reuters and Euronews.

  3. FIRST robots

    High school

    Competition robots for Team Integra 3646, which qualified for the FIRST Championship twice. They taught him to build reliable machines on hard deadlines, with a large team.

  4. Drones

    Custom builds

    An autonomous quadcopter with a carbon fiber frame and a Pixhawk flight controller, running OpenCV on a Raspberry Pi for navigation and onboard vision, and a window washing drone prototype with a pressure washer hose.

  5. Solar builds

    Ages 11 to 16

    A radio controlled solar car at 11, a portable solar generator at 12, a bedroom wired to run off a solar panel, and at 16 the welded aluminum frame of a full size solar car.
