About

Ragnar Robotics builds mobile robots that change their own tools and take on repetitive work. R2 loads and unloads CNC machines in machine shops. R1 paints, sands and drills walls in construction. Mini takes on light technician tasks.

R1 and R2 share one base with swappable tops. Ragnar was started in Miami in 2026 by aerospace engineer Onat Cakici.
