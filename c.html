Ragnar C, up close

One robot for the light, repetitive work inside a building: paint, sand, drill, mark, install small parts and check the result.

The machine

A base on two hub motor wheels, a three stage mast and a six axis arm. The base carries the paint, the battery and six tools; the mast lifts the arm; the arm swaps tools on its own.

6
  1. LiDAR. On top of the mast, it maps the room and finds walls, doors, trim and people.
  2. Three stage mast. Stows at 78.5 in and lifts the arm so tools reach 16 ft walls and 17 ft ceilings.
  3. Six axis arm. A collaborative arm with 16.5 lb payload, 37 in reach and force sensing at the wrist.
  4. Tool changer. Passes paint, air, vacuum and signals to the tool, and holds it without power.
  5. Tool station. Six docks on the deck: roller, sprayer, sander, drill, gripper and tape.
  6. Base. Two hub motor wheels and four casters. A lidar behind the glass band at the front stops it for obstacles.

Target specifications

Size and weight

Footprint
28 by 41 in (0.72 by 1.04 m) with the bumper; fits a 36 in door
Height, stowed
78.5 in (2.0 m); fits under an 80 in door
Work height
Walls to 16 ft (5.0 m), ceilings to 17 ft (5.3 m)
Weight
About 350 lb (159 kg) empty, 375 lb (170 kg) with a full tank

Motion

Drive
Two hub motor wheels and four casters
Lift
Three stage telescopic mast; the arm carriage travels 10.8 ft (3.3 m)
Arm
Six axis collaborative arm, 16.5 lb (7.5 kg) payload, 37 in (0.95 m) reach, force sensing

Tools and paint

Tools
Paint fed roller with edger pad, sprayer, sander with dust extraction, rotary hammer with vacuum clamp, gripper with palm camera, tape applicator
Tool change
Automatic, at six docks on the deck
Paint
2.6 gal (10 L) tank and pump in the base

Power and sensing

Power
One battery pack, swapped through the rear door
Sensing
360 degree LiDAR on the mast, a 2D lidar and a depth camera on the base, a front camera on the carriage, a wrist camera and a color sensor
Operator
10 in screen on the handle, plus a tablet
