News

Milestones on the way to our first pilots.

November 2026

Ragnar joins NVIDIA Inception

Ragnar Robotics has been accepted into NVIDIA Inception, the NVIDIA program for startups. It gives us access to NVIDIA's simulation and robotics tools as we move from design to a working robot.

November 2026

Ragnar M, our robot for machine shops

We completed the design of Ragnar M. It drives to the machine that needs it, unloads the finished part and loads the next one, and swaps print plates between jobs. It runs on the same base as Ragnar C.

See Ragnar M

October 2026

Ragnar C, our robot for construction

We completed the design of Ragnar C, which paints, sands and drills walls and ceilings, and simulated a full job with every move checked for collisions.

See Ragnar C

September 2026

Ragnar Robotics starts in Miami

Ragnar Robotics was founded in Miami to build mobile robot technicians that change their own tools.
