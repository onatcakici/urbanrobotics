R1, up close

One robot for the light, repetitive work inside a building: paint, sand, drill, mark, install small parts and check the result. Everything here comes from the CAD model and our simulations; R1 has not been built yet.

R1 at work

A 42 second film animated from the CAD model, with every move checked for collisions: R1 rolls in and maps the room, paints the walls, cuts the ceiling line, drills anchor holes, sands a patch and changes tools on its own. Then its top lifts off the base: one base, with tops for the trades that come next.

The machine

A swerve drive base, a three stage mast and a six axis arm. The base carries the paint, the batteries and six tools; the mast lifts the arm; the arm swaps tools on its own.

  1. LiDAR. On top of the mast, it maps the room and finds walls, doors, trim and people.
  2. Three stage mast. Stows at 2.0 m and lifts the arm so tools reach 16 ft walls and 17 ft ceilings.
  3. Six axis arm. A collaborative arm with 7.5 kg payload, 0.95 m reach and force sensing at the wrist.
  4. Tool changer. Passes paint, air, vacuum and signals to the tool, and holds it without power.
  5. Tool station. Six docks on the deck: roller, sprayer, sander, drill, gripper and tape.
  6. Base. Two swerve drive modules move it in any direction, including sideways along a wall.

Specifications

Size and weight

Footprint
0.72 by 1.04 m (28 by 41 in) with the bumper; fits a 36 in door
Height, stowed
2.0 m (78.5 in); fits under an 80 in door
Work height
Walls to 5.0 m (16 ft), ceilings to 5.3 m (17 ft)
Weight
About 174 kg (384 lb) empty, 186 kg (410 lb) with a full tank

Motion

Drive
Two swerve modules and four casters; drives sideways along a wall
Lift
Three stage telescopic mast; the arm carriage travels 3.3 m (10.8 ft)
Arm
Six axis collaborative arm, 7.5 kg (16.5 lb) payload, 0.95 m (37 in) reach, force sensing

Tools and paint

Tools
Paint fed roller with edger pad, sprayer, sander with dust extraction, rotary hammer with vacuum clamp, gripper with palm camera, tape applicator
Tool change
Automatic, at six docks on the deck
Paint
10 L (2.6 gal) tank and pump in the base

Power and sensing

Power
Two battery packs, swapped through the rear door
Sensing
360 degree LiDAR, four depth cameras on the base, a front camera on the carriage, a wrist camera and a color sensor
Operator
10 in screen on the handle, plus a tablet

Design targets from the current CAD model and our kinematics. The prototype has not been built yet, and these numbers will change as we test it.
