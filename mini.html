Mini, up close

Mini is the small one, built for data centers: it swaps drives and checks racks, and its round base fits tight aisles. Mini is in design. Everything on this page comes from the CAD model.

Inside

A round drive base that turns in its own footprint, a short lift and a collaborative arm with two grippers. The deck carries a tray for drives, and the base carries the battery, the computer and the arm controller.

  1. Arm. A Universal Robots UR7e: 7.5 kg (16.5 lb) payload and 850 mm (33.5 in) reach.
  2. Lift. An Ewellix LIFTKIT raises the arm up to 600 mm (23.6 in), for the high and the low slots in a rack.
  3. Two grippers. OnRobot 2FG7 grippers on a dual quick changer: one pulls the old drive, the other slides in the new one.
  4. Depth cameras. RealSense D455 front and rear, and a RealSense D405 on the wrist for close work.
  5. Safety scanners. Two SICK nanoScan3 safety laser scanners, front and rear.
  6. Computer. An NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin on board.
  7. Arm controller. The Universal Robots control box, inside the base.
  8. Battery. LiFePO4, 25.6 V and 100 Ah (2.6 kWh), in four modules.
  9. Drive. Two drive wheels on rockers, with casters front and rear.
