Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Urban Robotics Technologies, Inc. ("Ragnar Robotics", "we") collects through ragnarrobotics.com and how we use it. The short version: the site sets no cookies and has no advertising trackers. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not identify you. We only receive personal information when you send us a task through the form, email us or book a call with us.

What we collect

  • Tasks you send through the form. The form on the home page is delivered by FormSubmit, a form service, which emails us your answers, your email address and any file you attach.
  • Email you send us. If you write to hello@ragnarrobotics.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you put in the message.
  • Calls you book. The booking link opens Calendly, a scheduling service. If you book a call, we receive the name, the email address and any notes you enter there.
  • Server logs kept by our host. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operations. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages and links bring people to the site. It records the page you open, the site that sent you, your browser, device type and country, and how fast the page loaded. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not identify you. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We do not use cookies, session recording, chat widgets or advertising pixels on this site.

How we use it

We use the information in your email or booking to reply to you, to discuss a possible pilot, and to keep a record of our business conversations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the company, such as our email provider, FormSubmit, which delivers the form, and Cloudflare, which counts visits, and only as needed for that service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need them for the conversation and for normal business records. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@ragnarrobotics.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. This site does not track visitors over time or across other websites, so it works the same whether or not the signal is sent.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If we add other services in the future, we will describe them here before they go live.

Contact

Urban Robotics Technologies, Inc., Miami, Florida. Email hello@ragnarrobotics.com.
