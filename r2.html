R2, up close

R2 does the repetitive work in machine shops and print farms: it loads CNC machines, swaps print plates and moves parts between stations. It uses the same chassis as R1, with a lift and an arm on top instead of the mast.

Inside

The same chassis as R1, with a lift and a collaborative arm with two grippers on top. The chassis carries the batteries, the computer and the depth cameras, and the deck carries a cassette for raw stock and one for finished parts.

  1. Arm. A Universal Robots UR12e: 12.5 kg (27.6 lb) payload and 1.3 m (51 in) reach, to load a machine through its door.
  2. Lift. An Ewellix LIFTKIT raises the arm up to 900 mm (35 in), from low fixtures to the top shelf of a print farm.
  3. Two grippers. OnRobot 2FG7 grippers on a dual quick changer, so it can hold a raw part and a finished part in one visit.
  4. Depth cameras. Four RealSense D435 on the corners of the chassis see all around it. A RealSense D405 on the wrist checks every grip up close.
  5. Computer. Two compute modules and the electronics, inside the chassis.
  6. Battery. Two battery packs, swapped through the rear door.
  7. Drive. Two swerve drive modules and four casters, the same as R1. It can drive sideways along a row of machines.
  8. Cassettes. One for raw stock and one for finished parts, on the deck.
